hk widgets: the service `Widgets` of the package `hostkeeper.fixture.v1`. Widgets, for the tests.

Usage: hk [global flags] widgets <method> [flags]

Methods:
  make
      Make a widget from every kind of field.
  brand-new-method
      A method no real proto has: it appears in the script with no hand edit.
  login
      Log in with secrets.
  wait
      Hold the call for 'wait_secs', as Releases.WaitConverged does.
